# NeuralLoom

**Never lose the thread of your story.**

NeuralLoom is a creative knowledge workspace designed to help writers preserve, organize, understand, and continue their narrative projects.

Long creative projects accumulate characters, events, relationships, ideas, and decisions. Over time, keeping track of everything becomes difficult.

NeuralLoom aims to make that easier by helping creators maintain context across their projects.

## Vision

Build a workspace where writers can return to any project, understand what has happened, reconnect with their ideas, and continue creating without losing the thread.

## What We're Building

- **Project Memory:** Preserve important information across long creative projects.
- **Narrative Understanding:** Connect characters, events, relationships, and plotlines.
- **Project Reconstruction:** Help writers catch up on projects they haven't touched in a while.
- **Continuity Support:** Identify potential inconsistencies and forgotten details.
- **Evidence-Based Answers:** Connect insights to the original source material.
